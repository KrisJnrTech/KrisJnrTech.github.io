Solar Installation Process

A good installation starts with planning and correct system design before the equipment is connected.

1

Assess

Determine the energy requirements, loads, available space and system objectives.

2

Design

Select appropriate panels, inverter, battery capacity, wiring and protection.

3

Install

Install and connect the equipment according to the equipment manuals and applicable requirements.

4

Test

Verify connections, polarity, voltages, protection and system operation before use.

⚠️ Solar Installation Safety

  • Always follow the manufacturer's installation and wiring instructions.
  • DC solar circuits can remain energized whenever panels are exposed to light.
  • Use appropriately rated conductors, connectors and protective devices.
  • Cable sizing should consider both ampacity and voltage drop.
  • A cable calculator provides a design estimate; the final installation should also be checked against the cable manufacturer's specifications and applicable electrical requirements.
